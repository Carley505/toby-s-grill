Our Story

Hospitality Straight From The Heart

At Toby's Grill, we believe that great food tastes even better when served with genuine warmth. As a family-run restaurant in the heart of Maweni, Nyali, our owners personally greet every guest who steps through our black gate.

Whether you're stopping by for a casual date night, bringing the family for sizzled burgers, or ordering your favorite steak for home delivery, our kitchen fires up every plate with passion. Don't forget to try our fresh signature chimichurri sauce — and don't be surprised if a complimentary warm chocolate brownie with vanilla ice cream finds its way to your table!
